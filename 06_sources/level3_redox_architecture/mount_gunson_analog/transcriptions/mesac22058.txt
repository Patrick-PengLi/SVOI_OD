INTRODUCTION

The following report summarizes investigations carried out in SML 527
during the three months 4 December 1970 to 3 March 1971.

Because mich of the previous drilling on this lease was on one or other of
two local grids, with a local level datum, all coordinates and levels were re -
calculated relative to State metric values. The summaries for all drilling on this
area, including recent drilling, are attached as appendix (1). Structural
investigations based on the earlier drilling are under way at present. A reassessment
of the Main Open Cut is in progress. The investigation of the West Lagoon Area was not
carried any further this period.

Several percussion drill holes were put down in the areg of the House Workings
to gain further information on this body.

In the area roughly bounded by the edge of Pematty Lagoon at the Mystery
_ Workings; the House Workings; the Main Open Cut and the Gunyot Workings, henceforth
called the Gunyot Mystery Area, a series of drill traverses were carried out, the
results of which are being assessed.

Three drill traverses were made across the Gunyah Lake to obtain structural and
geochemical information.

The blocks of dolomite at the Sweet Nell Mines and the adjacent block ( Pair Nell
Mines?) were drilled to test whether ecenomic copper is present.

A total of about 53 targets were placed in preparation for aerial photography.

In appendix (i) figures are given for the value of "ore" intersections.
These are calculated at a cut ~ off grade of 0.4% Cu. This cut - off grade is
independent of the type of mineralisation and is used solely as a means of comparing
the relative values of different areas. It is not an economic parameter,

REASSESSMENT OF EARLIER INVESTIGATIONS

Earlier drilling on this property had been based on two different grid systems.
It was necessary to bring all grid values into the one system before converting these
to the State metric grid. This programme is now complete, with the exception of
about three drill holes of unknom position. The drilling summarized in appendix (i)

includes the new values. ‘

In order to find the values of the mine grid in terms of the State system
it was necessary to perform a triangulation survey over the northern part of SML 527
and the eastern part of SML 494. This proceedure will be used later on the southern
part of SML 527 in order to locate the true position of other investigations.

Maps have been drawn up at a scale of 1:5000 to show the collar locations of
all exploration drill holes with the exception of the lagoon areas, where larger scale
drawings are used,

Many of the old drill holes were put down as part of a biogeochemical
investigation, and noxaccurate survey was made of the collar location, In these cases
we have made a “best estimate" of the location, this being considered of sufficient

accuracy.
WEST LAGOON OREBODY

The investigation drilling into the extent of ore grade mineralisation in the
West Lagoon was completed early in December. The location of these holes is shom on
map No. 12 and is included in the summaries.

Time did not allow for the third stage drilling mentioned in our previous
report, it being decided to use blast hole drilling as part of the sampling programme,

The cross sections mentioned in the last report are still in the working
drawing stage, and are thus not in a suitable format for sending out.

HOUSE WORKINGS.

In view of the decreased price of copper at present, it has been considered
necessary to re - assess the ore reserves for all orebodies. As part of this exercise
6 preliminary drill holes were put down in this area. The interim results of these
holes are show in the summaries.

GUNYOT - MYSTERY AREA INVESTIGATIONS

A series of 26 percussion drill holes were put down to test the area between the
House Workings and Pernatty Lagoon. A twofold purpose was intended, in order to obtain
stratigraphic information and to get an idea of any mineralisation in that region,
Interim results of this drilling is show in the summaries.

GUNYAH LAKE INVESTIGATIONS

The drilling here was carried out without intersecKing any ore bodies.
The Gunyah Lake has been shown to be a shallow basin type structure, but an
important factor is the absence of any sign of the Woocalla Dolomite, The drill
results are shown in the summaries,

ZRONSTONE LAGOON INVESTIGAPTONS

A total of 44 percussion drill holes was achieved on the Sweet Nell, the
Fair Nell and on an ironstone outcrop to the east of the Sweet Nell.

The "shale" unit under the Woocalla Dolomite cont<ins copper, but the minerals
have not yet been identified. It is probable that the relation between carbonate and
sulphide mineralisation is dependent on water table conditions in Ironstone Lagoon.

Since all survey of this lagoon is on a local grid until triangulation is
complete, no final plans have been prepared.

AERTAL PHOTOGRAPHY .

A total of 46 large targets were placed for the complete photographic
coverage in colour of the S.M.L., and 7 small targets for a low level coverage of the
Main Open Cut and House Workings.

These targets will be co-ordinated as soon as practicable.

INTRODUCTION

Ouring the last three months there has been extensive
activity in many areas of the SML.
At times there have been two percussion drills in operation

to expedite exploration,

Mapping at a scale of approximately 1:17200 has commenced

in two areas of the SML.

Aerial photography was carried out in early March and some

photos have been received.

EAST LAGOON WORKINGS 3 - 1

Known mineralisation on the northern and western side of
the pit were drilled to test whether any more ore grade material was
available, In addition some holes were drilled in the bottom of the

pit to test for underlying mineralisation.

The results of the drilling have not yet been assessed.
Since the drilling summaries and plan attached are on the old mine

grid and datum, all values given are in feet.

HOUSE WORKINGS 3 —- 3 & MAIN OPEN CUT WORKINGS 3 ~ 4

Extensive drilling programmes were undertaken to test the
areas previously shown as ore reserves. Many drill cuttings await
analysis, but visual logging suggests that mineralisation is less

extensive and lower grade than was formerly thought.

The drilling summaries attached are on State metric values,
as are all documents with the exception of the East Lagoon. The drill

hole locations are shown on the accompanying plans.

GUNYOT WORKINGS 3-5

This area has been redrilled to re-evaluate the carbonate

copper reserves, Full results of assays are not yet available.

A drilling summary and location plan are attached.

PERNATTY LAGOON — MYSTERY AREA 3-6

Some drilling has been commenced along the eastern and
north eastern margins of Pernatty Lagoon in this area, in order
to test for mineralisation similar to the other lagoon ore bodies
and to see if the known mineralisation at the Mystery Shaft has any

extension,

Great difficulty has been experienced with moving on the
lagoon,mud, or in trenches, due to wet conditions (for this region).
The drilling programme for this area is incomplete, and will be

finished later.

GUNYOT — MYSTERY AREA 3 - 7

A series of drill traverses have been carried out across
the depression to the north of the House Workings and the Main Open
Cut Workings. The drilling was intended to both define structure and
to test for extensions of the known mineralisation. As far as can be

known at present, no significant mineralisation was encountered.

A drilling summary and drill hole location sheet is

included.

1. INTROOUCTION :

There has been continued exploration activity in the
SML during the last three months, using both drilling and geophysical methods

as well as surface mapping.

The drilling has been concentrated in the northern
part of the lease, but some programmes have been carried out in dolomite
areas at the old Manganese Workings, the Sweet Nell Mines and near the
Woocalla Quarry. Far the last area permission for drilling was sought and
obtained from the Commonwealth Railways to carry out the drilling.

Three geophysical techniques have been used, ground
E.M., proton recession magnetometer and I.P. The first technique appears to
have possibilities on the lagoon areas, Generally the magnetometer appears
to be of little use in this area, Previous experience with I.P. has shown its
value in the shallow lagoon workings and the dry land deposits. Part of the
present exercise is to test its applicability to deeper investigation in

the lagoon.

Mapping at 1:17200 has continued throughout the
lease, while more detailed mapping at 1:5000 is being carried out in the
vicinity of the knewn workings.

The aerial photographs by the Department of Lands were
received, and are being used in exploration,

2. HOUSE_WORKINGG 3-3

The results of the drilling carried out in this area
have been evaluated, and all previous drilling results have been combined
with the latest on two drawings Nos. 235 and 240, These drawings and a new
copy of the drilling summary are included with this report.

Further drilling may be carried out in this area prior

to any decision on mining.

24th August, 1971.

Test Magnetic Survey, Mt. Gunson — Project 23

During July 1971, Austral Exploration Services Pty Ltd read 4
traverses with proton precession magnetometer. The location of these
traverses and the magnetic profiles along them are shown on Figures 1 and
2 respectively.

The traverses were chosen so as to cover several types of mineral
occurrence and to test correlation with results from other geophysical methods.
A proton precession magnetometer was chosen because previous magnetic work in
the area (e.g. work over manganese mineralisation in the Pernatty Lagoon are’ >
by McPharlin, 1949) had indicated a need for an instrument sensitivity of re a
gamma. -

Most of the large amplitude disturbance on the profiles is due to
drill rods and pipes. One not labelled but which is almost certainly due
to this catise is located about 475 and 575 on Traverse 1. The only
significant anomaly is that at about 600-665 on traverse 38, It coincides with
a weak I.P. indication and is interpreted as due to a flat body, or one dipping
shallowly to the north. This interpretation fits in with the geological facts
as known at present and testing of it can be included in the currently planned
drilling programme in this area. Holes nearby to 40 ft and 25 ft indicated
oxidized copper but the hole to test the magnetic anomaly should be drilled to
75 ft vertical depth.

Some less distinct anomalies of small amplitude, at 34/1625 and
38/2450 may be significant. Whilst these indications do not stand out they
may represent the type of anomaly which is associated with mineralisation. A
shallow hole near 34/1625 contained 0.98% Cu (oxidized); a hole, depth 30 ft,
near 3B/2450 indicated up to 1% oxidized copper. Both magnetic anomalies indicate
bodies at shallow depth. Other distumbarices on the profiles are not significant ~
they are due to geological noise and are contained within an envelope of reading
error,

Conclusion

Except possibly for the instances given, the magnetic method has
not proved suitable as an exploration technique. The anomaly about 600-665
on traverse 38 should be tested by drilling and the core examined for magnetic
properties. Some further magnetic work should be done about the two minor
anomalies cited above before testing is done on them.

2. Geological Setting

Regionally, copper deposits of Mt. Gunson lie in an inlier
of older Pandurra Formation (here termed the "window'') projecting
through overlying, disconformable Woocalla Dolomite. In turn the
"window" is partly capped by the Tent Hill Formation, which lies

disconformably over both the lower rock units.

The window of Pandurra Formation, protruding through Woocalla
Dolomite, répresents a north=trending structural culmination of the
gently-folded rock Sequence. Within this window,known economic concentr-
ations of copper as seen in plan jare restricted by the following

3 Ore Genesis

The Source of Copper

ahe
It is suggested that, ultimate source of the copper metal

was, dispersed syngenetic sourcef in the Woocalla Dolomite, This
formation contains anomalously high amounts of heavy metals, including
copper. On the evidence from a small number of geochemical rock analyses,
the formation appears to be especially rich in copper around the areas

now being worked and worked in the past,i.e. at the Mt. Gunson Mine

area and at Sweet Nell and Monalena, east and south of Woocalla.

The Concentration to Copper Ore

The process responsible for the concentration of copper
into economic bodies is envisaged as having been a residual upgrading
of syngenetic Cu by dissolution under weathering or other Bubssore
processes, and remeval in solution of dolomite Sm the rock body
exposed by uplift along the Pernatty Culmination. The now upgraded
remaining copper rich material is concentrated in depressions in the

disconformable, pre-Woocalla surface,with the flanking, unweathered*

dolomite formation providing a precipitation barrier. The same

Feasibility of the Concentration Mechanism

Copper values of 300 to 500 p.p.m. have been recorded in
unmineralised dolomite in areas near copper workings. Lower values

were obtained in the area of the main manganese mines.

A content of 300 p.p.em. of Cu in dolomite, is equivalent to

a grade of 0.6 pounds of Cu per short ton of rock.

Using a figure of 14 cubic feet of dolomite to the ton,
1 eubiec foot of rock would provide 0.6 pounds of Cu metal.

Three milion tons of 1% Cu Mineralization in the Gunson
area represents 60 x 10° pounds of copper metal. Therefore 14 x 10°
wubic feet (approx. 5 x 407 cubic yards) of dolomite would be required

to be dissolved to release the quantity of copper contained in the

Gunson deposits if it were originally syngenetic copper.

If an average thickness of 30 feet is taken for the dolomite,
the area of rock that would have to be dissolved to release sufficient
copper to form the Gunson deposits would be about 1.5 square miles

(only about twice the area of the Gunyah Lake), an area forming but

a fraction of the weathered and dissected "window" space.

4, Recommendations on Future Exploration

On the basis of the foregoing hypothesis and also on the
basis of paleogeographic similarity, the most favourable areas for
future copper exploration would be on the exposed and concealed contact
of the dolomite facies of the Woocalla Formation along the eastern

and southern rims of the "window' of Pandurra Formation.

Further concentration of exploration effort could be effécted

in two ways:

a) By systematic rock-chip geochemical sampling of the outcropping
dolomite, or shallow sample drilling, to test for haloes of highest
copper concentration within the original rock body.

b) By concentrating sampling in areas of low present relief, which

possibly represent erosional depressions in the pre-Tent Hill suirface.

Three prime targets are suggested by this hypothesis (Figure 3).

These are in order of interest:

4) The concealed western edge of the dolomite between the original

Main Open Cut workings and the Gunyot workings.

2) The Dolomite ~ Pandurra contact in Lake Dutton, near South Point.

3) The Sweet-Nell area, especially beneath Ironstone Lagoon.

Mr. McManus has indicated a fourth target (which also fits
the concepts presented here) in an area to the north of the manganese
workings, where the Dolomite - Pandurra contact is concealed by Tent
Hill Formation.
