# Pre-1975 analog literature: what is here and what is still to supply (2026-09-21)

Annex A table row 3 cites "Mendelsohn 1961; Kupferschiefer literature" for the
reduced-facies belt and patch lengths (tens of km; 1 to 10 km), and Level 4
uses the same set plus the White Pine papers. Only editions printed before
t0 = 2 May 1975 count.
