NOTES OK PROPOSED DRILL SITES - MIDDLEBACK RANGE D.M. 776/52
                                                 Rept. Bk No 35/31

         Following recommendations for additional drilling over the
Katunga Hills region of the Middleback Range (D.M. 776/52, 2/2/53),
J. McCahon, Departmental surveyor-draftsman, has completed survey-
ing the suggested grid base lines and fixed the bore sites
according to the approved recommendations.
         On 18 th, 19th February 1953 the writer inspected the sites
in the field, and made notes on the topographical and geological
conditions at each site.                                               '
