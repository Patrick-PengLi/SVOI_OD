# annexA_transcriptions

Hand transcriptions of period documents, one CSV per table, each row with
its report page and PDF page so it can be checked against the scan.

- `risely1968_specific_gravity.csv`: Risely, RB 67/00081, "Geophysical
  report no. 1 on a gravity and magnetic survey of the Roopena Volcanics",
  1 Nov 1968, surface-sample specific gravities, report p. 11 (PDF p. 14).
  Group means are as printed beside the table.
- `risely1968_sadm_hole_logs.csv`: the summarized logs of the two SADM
  diamond holes in the same report, p. 5 (PDF p. 8); feet as printed,
  metres converted (x 0.3048). These are Roopena DDH 1 and 2 of SA Geodata.
- `risely1968_text.txt`: the report's text layer page by page (uncorrected), for quoting.
Transcribed 2026-09-21 from the report's text layer and checked against
the page.
