# WMC targeting memoranda: the TEXT is NOT FOUND; the 1974 targeting PLANS are (checked 2026-09-21)

Annex A names "the WMC targeting memoranda" (Haynes) as a basis for Level 2
(basalt-object intensity, lineament multiplier) and Level 4 (ore rule, reach d).

Searched: the SARIG catalogue (all WMC / Western Mining / Stuart Shelf /
Andamooka / Roxby Downs records) and the WP1 acquisition index for anything
WMC wrote before t0 (2 May 1975). No memorandum text is in open file; the
memoranda are internal WMC papers (now BHP's archive), the inventory's S22.

What survives from WMC's 1974 targeting, found on the faces of O'Driscoll's
sheets (GA eCat 79287), in `../odriscoll_interpretation/window_sheets/`:
- OD6 iv, "Andamooka & Torrens summary plan for the meeting 30/9/74 -
  5/10/74 to select targets", Sept 1974: the BLUE "T.O'D targets (1974)" are
  in state; the RED "D.McP.D targets (1976)" and the tenement notes dated to
  Aug 1975 were added later. Blue circles transcribed in
  `../odriscoll_interpretation/odriscoll_OD6iv_1974_blue_targets.csv`.
- OD7 i, "Stuart Shelf exploration plan 2616-01, Andamooka area", Sept 1974,
  with tectonic targets and a WMC proposed drill hole. In state.
These plans are the period record of WHERE WMC targeted and why (anomaly
coincidence at lineament intersections); they do not state the basalt-donor
rule in words.

Also here, labelled:

  the Mount Gunson Cattle Grid model, not the basalt-donor concept.
- finding_aid_not_admissible/: Haynes (2006, SEG), retrospective; locates

suggests a copper environment "immediately beneath the volcanics" at the
Roopena Volcanics / Corunna Conglomerate unconformity: an in-state SADM
statement of part of the concept.
