# The exploration concept as WMC wrote it: EL 190 first quarterly report

Source: mesac24890 (SARIG data release, EL 190 Andamooka progress reports,
515 pp, image only). Report title page (PDF p. 4): "Exploration Licence 190,
Andamooka, Quarterly Report 2 May - 2 August, 1975, Western Mining
Corporation Limited". Transcribed by eye from 200 dpi renders on 2026-09-10;
page crops in `el190_q1_pages/`. PDF page = "m90 p" numbering used across the
project.

Standing under the WP4 citation gate: this is the EARLIEST written statement
of the concept on disk. It is dated after the EL 190 grant (2 May 1975) and
was written in the quarter in which RDD1 was drilled and completed (RDD1
"has just been completed" in section 2.1). It therefore enters the pre-RD1
state only as QUALIFIED (the licence and its stated purpose pre-date RD1; the
document does not) and the t0 state not at all.

## 1.2 Geological Environment (m90 p. 7), verbatim

> The licence was obtained to document the stratigraphic section present in
> the northern portion of the Stuart Shelf. Specific interest was to
> ascertain whether suitable environments for stratiform copper existed. The
> presence of the Cattle Grid Deposit (Mt. Gunson Mines Pty. Ltd.) at the
> Pandurra Formation - Whyalla Sandstone unconformity was used as one possible
> model.
>
> The area under title had not previously been explored for base metals.
> This is probably largely due to difficult exploration conditions consisting
> of poor access, Quaternary sand cover, flat-lying Upper Proterozoic
> stratigraphy overlain, in part, by Cambrian and Cretaceous rocks.

## 1.1 Land Tenure (m90 p. 7), verbatim in part

> Exploration Licence 190 was granted on 2 May, 1975, for a one year period.
> The licence covers 2358 km2 within the pastoral stations of Roxby Downs and
> Andamooka. It is located wholly within Andamooka SH-53-12. [...]
> Expenditure commitment for the licence is a minimum of $15,000 within six
> months and $50,000 within twelve months from the date of granting, to be
> spent on geophysical surveys and drilling.

## 2.2 Geophysical, stated purposes (m90 pp. 7-8), verbatim in part

> 2.2.1 Seismic Survey. Prior to the initiation of drilling, a
> refraction/reflection seismic survey was conducted to define the depth to
> basement. This was done as a control on the depth required in the
> stratigraphic drill hole. [...]
>
> 2.2.2 Magnetic Traverse. One 24.0 kilometer long ground magnetic traverse
> was conducted along 321.5 deg magnetic north through Olympic Dam. The
> traverse was designed to provide data to enable a depth calculation to the
> top of a magnetic anomaly interpreted as possible basement.

## 3.2.2 Magnetics (m90 p. 9), verbatim

> The ground magnetic traverse yielded a profile almost identical to that
> defined by airborne magnetics. This similarity is interpreted to indicate
> that the source of the magnetic anomaly is at substantial depth.

## What this settles and what it does not

- The concept WMC put on paper at the licence is STRATIFORM COPPER with the
  Mount Gunson Cattle Grid deposit at the Pandurra Formation - Whyalla
  Sandstone unconformity as "one possible model". That is the admissible
  period statement of the host class for WP4 Task 4.
- The altered-basalt source, the fault conduit and the lineament targeting
  of Haynes (2006) do NOT appear in this statement. On the record as it
  stands they remain testimony (inventory rows S20 to S22).
- The first hole's bottom 75 m was logged in the field as "red hematitic
  amygdaloidal basic volcanic", i.e. as a basalt. The concept was not
  rejected at RD1 in this document; the intersection was read as the
  predicted source rock. This bears directly on the Task 6 expansion rule.
- The stated purpose of the pre-drilling geophysics was depth to basement
  (seismic) and depth to the magnetic source (ground traverse), not target
  discrimination.
- "The area under title had not previously been explored for base metals"
  is WMC's own statement of the S11 to S15 question (prior licences over
  the ground): to be reconciled with the Roxby Downs 1966-67 licence
  (mesac2301).
