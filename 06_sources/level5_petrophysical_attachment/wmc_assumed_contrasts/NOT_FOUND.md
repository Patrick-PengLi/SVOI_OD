# The WMC model sheets: NOT FOUND (checked 2026-09-22)

Annex A asks for "the density contrasts the WMC interpreters themselves assumed
when modeling the anomalies". WMC's own sample measurements and model sheets
from 1974-75 are not in open file: the work pre-dates the licence, so it sits in
WMC's files (now BHP's) or in the EL 190 application papers. Same gap as
Haynes's memoranda (inventory S22); one decision covers both.

What IS here:
  Rutter_Esdale_1985_ExplGeophys_16_273.pdf  The two WMC geophysicists' own
      account (Expl. Geophys. 16, 273-276): Roopena Volcanics sampled at Roopena
      Station and Depot Creek against five Marinoan units; susceptibility
      contrast "variable"; density contrast 0.3 gms/cc "established with a
      greater degree of credibility"; block models gave Olympic Dam 1,150 m
      (850 m if reshaped) and a magnetic source at about 2,000 m.
      DATED 1985, i.e. AFTER t0. It records a pre-t0 act, as Haynes (2006) does.
      RULING NEEDED on its standing.
  pre_t0_stand_ins/risely1968_text.txt  SADME's own 1968 specific gravities from
      the same Roopena outcrops: Roopena Volcanic 2.61-3.35 (means 2.68-2.87),
      weathered 2.42, Pandurra quartzite 2.44, Moonabie quartzite 2.62-2.66,
      reduction density 2.67. A Roopena-against-Pandurra contrast of about 0.35.
  pre_t0_stand_ins/rb6300075  Milton et al. 1966 at Mount Gunson: reduction
      density 2.59, residual contrast over the ore only 0.3 mGal, and seven
      methods with "no diagnostic relationships to the ore occurrence".

All of it is transcribed, with pages, in era_textbooks/level5_era_properties.csv.
